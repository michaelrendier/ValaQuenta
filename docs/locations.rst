Elsewhere in the repository
===========================

The API Reference documents the importable package: the standalone engines,
``engine/`` and ``modules/``. The rest of the repository is not part of the
package and is not autodoc'd. It lives here:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Location
     - What it holds
   * - `wiki/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/wiki>`_
     - The record: one results page per engine, retractions, and
       ``results_at_a_glance.md``.
   * - `wiki/toolbox/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/wiki/toolbox>`_
     - Every equation of every registry module, with parameters, confidence
       tier and a worked call. Generated from the manifests and formularies.
   * - `notebooks/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/notebooks>`_
     - The derivation notebooks, grouped by engine, tier and topic.
   * - `ZeroLattice/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/ZeroLattice>`_
     - The four Telperion paper notebooks.
   * - `code/noether_engine/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/code/noether_engine>`_
     - A standalone general Noether current engine (its own README,
       ``architecture.md`` and tests). See its ``PENDING_MIGRATION.md``.
   * - `code/wiles_modularity/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/code/wiles_modularity>`_
     - The modularity theorem, computationally.
   * - `code/sonification/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/code/sonification>`_
     - Sonification scripts. See its ``PENDING_MIGRATION.md``.
   * - `code/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/code>`_
     - Standalone experiments: ``hypercomplex_zeta.py``, ``io_polar.py``,
       ``clay_rh_radians.js``.
   * - `addenda/udeo_crypto/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/addenda/udeo_crypto>`_
     - The UDEO write-up and its proof-of-concept scripts.
   * - `examples/hello_lens/ <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/tree/main/examples/hello_lens>`_
     - A reference ``lens`` plugin: the smallest working example of the
       ValaQuenta Format.
   * - `FORMAT.md <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/blob/main/FORMAT.md>`_
     - The plugin format specification (``valaquenta.plugin/1``) and its
       `JSON schema <https://github.com/michaelrendier/ValaQuenta/blob/main/schema/valaquenta-plugin-1.schema.json>`_.
