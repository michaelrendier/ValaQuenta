Hypergon constructibility: interactive visualizations

ValaQuenta engine 4, modules/hypergon_constructibility, with the n-ball of bell_curve. Four pages, each checked against the Python engines on load. No libraries, no network.

The sixteen hypergons

canvas2d_

Gauss–Wantzel round the sedenion ring: 4 of the 16 positions are constructible; click one for its polygon and the φ(n) test; test any n.

The Niemeier gap

canvas2d_

The 23 root systems of rank 24 by h mod 16, the three empty columns {1, 11, 15}, the Moonshine primes, the proof as data, and the exact control (68% of prime sets do the same).

The n-ball and the Gamma function

canvas2d_

Volume and sphere area at every real dimension, the peak at n = 5.257, the circle at n = 2, and |Γ| with its poles.

A 3D Cartesian frame (OpenSCAD companion)

canvas2d_

The n-ball curve in the frame (n, V, S) and the Niemeier gap as bars, orbitable, with the OpenSCAD source and renders.
