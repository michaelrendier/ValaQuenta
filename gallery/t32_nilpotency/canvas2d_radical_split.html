The square law: every word is nilpotent or an involutory unit

Every word w of the T32/GF(2) algebra of dimension d has w² = 0 or w² = e₀; nothing else occurs. At d = 8 the 256 words split 128 | 128. A picture shows a structure; it is not evidence that the structure means anything beyond its definition.
