(2) A lone component is invisible to sigma_rb and sits at sigma_self = ½; a ⊕4 pair gives sigma_self 1 (in phase) or 0 (antiphase). (3) sigma_self = ½ + Σ tilt/(p_red + p_blue); feeding Δ = 0.000707 into the tilt gives ½ + Δ/E whatever the struts do, so it lands on the sigma_riemann = ½ facet. (4) σ_self = ½ is a saddle of the gear flow with Lyapunov rate ≈ 6.1. The exploratory lifts (S1–S7) are not the documented map; they found the 84 zero-divisor directions of the sedenion and no feature at Δ, and are kept only as exploratory data. The scalar balance E_red − E_blue of sigma_rb_term does not contain sigma_riemann (finding VQ-9).
