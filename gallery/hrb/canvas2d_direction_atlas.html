Direction atlas: what the 15 imaginary components do

Input: one sedenion sigma_rb = sigma_riemann·e₀ + 15 imaginary components. Its imaginary part is a direction û on the sphere S¹⁴. Under lift B (Ĥ_RB = L_C, Ĥ_BR = R_C) the current balance is β·ad_û with ad_û = L_û − R_û. This page classifies every direction the algebra singles out.

Exploratory. This page uses one of two lifts of sigma_rb that were written for this gallery on 2026-11-25. They are not the documented map. The documented sigma_rb map is sigma_rb[k] = ψ[k]·conj ψ[k⊕4] (16 in, 16 out): see pages R1–R9.

Reading: the 15 basis directions e₁…e₁₅ are interchangeable (ad has rank 14, all singular values 2 — checked for each). Of the 210 directions (eᵢ ± eⱼ)/√2, exactly 84 are zero divisors (L_û has nullity 4, ad_û has rank 10): the known 84. The other 126 behave like basis directions. Random directions on S¹⁴ (4,000 sampled) are never special: ad has rank 14 with a continuum of singular values. So the facets are not the 15 components as such: only the zero-divisor directions are structurally different.
