ADD:SCALE:SIGN: interactive visualizations

ValaQuenta engine 6, modules/add_scale_sign (0.3). Five pages, each recomputing its numbers in the browser and comparing them with the Python engine. A picture shows a structure; it is not evidence that the structure means anything beyond its definition.

The commutator x⁻¹y⁻¹xy walked on a number line; it is always a pure translation, a·(1/s − 1) for ADD with SCALE and −2a for ADD with SIGN.
