Bracketing, firing order and set membership: interactive visualizations

ValaQuenta engine 7, modules/bracketing_firing_order (0.2). Five pages, each recomputing its numbers in the browser and comparing them with the Python engine. A picture shows a structure; it is not evidence that the structure means anything beyond its definition.

The evolving sedenion: input to output

canvas2d_

A 16-component input on e₀…e₁₅ and the vector after every step of a plan, under A, B, C and D, in the operator and the element reading; Ĥ_RB's coupling is one of the items.

Every plan of one kind, and which agree

canvas2d_

All plans of A, B, C or D over 3 or 4 items with their outputs; frames mark plans that give the same vector; item sets where plans collapse.

A plan as a tree

canvas2d_

Leaves, brackets and numbered firing order; click a bracket to change its order; the page names the kind (A, B, C or D).

How many plans: the counts to sixteen items

canvas2d_

A, B, C, D, Bell(n) and 2ⁿ⁻¹ on a log scale with exact values (BigInt); D contains A, B and C.

Set membership: has it been here before?

canvas2d_

Recamán's flip as a membership test, words of callables with their collisions and the flip test, and the jurisdiction check.
