## The Null Operator

`e₀ = 1` is the multiplicative identity — **The Null Operator**. It never
participates in zero-divisor crossings and is the reference axis of the
Cayley-Dickson tower.

When an engine returns the identity, or `V(0)=1`, or an unchanged value, **that
is the result, not a missing one.** NULL-as-identity: nothing × nothing = p.
The prime IS a singularity in factor space — its identity is its negative
space, what it cannot be.

Engines where this convention is load-bearing:
[singularity_null.md](singularity_null.md),
[fixed_point.md](fixed_point.md),
[telperion.md](telperion.md) (e₀ as the M87\* spin axis),
