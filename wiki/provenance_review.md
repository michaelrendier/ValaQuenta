# Provenance review

For the author. Each registry module's manifest now carries a machine-drafted `provenance.origin` and `created`
(the first-commit date in this repository, not necessarily the date the idea began), marked `_draft`. What the
drafts cannot say is the per-component label of the skill: **ESTABLISHED** (published work, cited and pointed at),
**OURS** (the author's construction), **FIRST STATED HERE**, and the `:CALCULATED` suffix. This sheet lists, for
each module, the published names its own docstrings and wiki page mention, as evidence for that call.

Set the label in the manifest (`provenance.status_label`), delete `_draft` and `_scaffolded` when a row is reviewed.
